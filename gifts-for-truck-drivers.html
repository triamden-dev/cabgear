Gifts for Truck Drivers They'll Actually Use

By Clifford Cruickshank, CDL-A driver · Updated November 2026

Disclosure: The mugs and tumblers in this guide are my own designs, sold in the TriAmden Marketing Etsy shop, and I earn from those sales. The other ideas link to guides on this site. Nothing else here pays me.

Buying for a driver is tricky. The cab is small, everything has to earn its spot, and most of us already have a phone mount. The best gifts solve a problem we deal with every shift: eating well, sleeping well and keeping the cab under control.

Before you buy: three questions

  • What does the truck already have? Some trucks come with a fridge and microwave built in. My 2022 Kenworth T680 has both, so a second fridge would be wasted space.
  • Where would it go? Ask about the space, or measure it on home time. A great gift that doesn't fit ends up in the garage.
  • How does it get power? Most cab gear runs on 12V. Anything that needs a regular wall plug also needs an inverter in the truck.

1. A hot meal without a truck stop

If the truck has no microwave, a 12V heated lunch box changes how a driver eats. Pack a home-cooked meal, plug it in a few hours before the break, and it's hot when you stop.

I compared two popular ones here: HOTLOGIC vs TRAVELISIMO.

2. Cold food that lasts the whole run

A 12V fridge lets a driver keep a week of real food instead of living on gas-station snacks. It's the bigger-ticket gift on this list, so check the space and the power draw first.

Use this before you buy: 12V fridge buying checklist.

3. Better sleep on a 10-hour break

Sleep is the thing drivers lose first. Blackout curtains for the bunk, a good eye mask, earplugs and a pillow from home all help. None of it is exciting. All of it gets used every night.

If the truck has an EPU or idles overnight, read My EPU Keeps Waking Me Up before you shop. It'll tell you what kind of noise you're fighting.

4. A cab that stays organized

A visor organizer for the paperwork and a steering wheel tray for meals and the laptop are cheap, small and used every day. My picks are in the cab kitchen and clutter guide, and the full system is in Organize Your Cab.

5. Something to drink from that fits the cupholder

Every driver goes through coffee, and every driver has a story about the EPU, reheated meals or the HOS clock. That's where my own designs come in:

  • Black 20oz insulated tumbler for the cupholder
  • White 40oz tumbler with a handle and straw for a full shift of water or iced coffee
  • 11oz black mug for home time

The EPU Collection is the one drivers laugh at first: "Dear EPU, Please Let Me Sleep" is the prayer every trucker with an EPU says before going to bed. Everything is made to order and ships free in the US. See all the designs on Etsy.

6. When you're not sure

A gift card for the truck stop chain they use most covers meals, snacks and supplies on the road. It's not personal, but it's never wasted.

What to skip

  • Anything big or heavy. Space and weight both matter.
  • Glass or anything fragile. It won't survive the first pothole.
  • Anything that needs a wall plug when the truck has no inverter.
  • Another phone mount. We have one. We probably have three.

From one driver: the best gift is the one that makes the next shift a little easier.
