About Cab Gear

Life on the road isn't just about driving. It's about making a truck feel like home, staying organized, eating well, getting proper rest, and finding ways to make every mile a little more comfortable.

I'm Clifford Cruickshank, a long-haul truck driver and founder of the TriAmden Group of Companies.

My journey didn't begin behind the wheel. I spent over 16 years working in the oil and gas industry in Trinidad and Tobago before moving to the United States in December 2016. In 2017, I began my trucking career with Knight Transportation.

Since then, I've driven for several carriers, hauling container, refrigerated and dry van freight on OTR and regional routes across the country. Today, my home away from home is a 2022 Kenworth T680 sleeper.

Over the years, I've learned that the little things make a big difference. A well-organized cab, the right equipment, a decent meal, or something as simple as getting an uninterrupted night's sleep can completely change your experience on the road.

That's why I created Cab Gear, published by TriAmden Marketing.

This isn't just another website listing trucking accessories. It's a place to share practical ideas, useful gear, firsthand experiences, and honest opinions about what works, what doesn't, and what could be better.

From cab organization and food storage to cooking, comfort, and everyday life behind the wheel, the goal is simple: help fellow drivers make smarter choices without wasting time or money.

I don't claim to have all the answers, and I certainly haven't tried every product out there. But with over a million miles behind the wheel, I've picked up plenty of lessons worth passing along.

No gimmicks. No unnecessary hype. Just real-world trucking experience, one mile at a time.
